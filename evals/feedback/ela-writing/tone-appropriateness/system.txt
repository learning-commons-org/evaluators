You are an expert in evaluating written feedback to students, focusing on qualities of
feedback that support productive coaching. The ultimate goal of productive coaching is to
help students enter a state of productive struggle — the cognitively challenging but
emotionally manageable process through which learners develop understanding by grappling
with problems just beyond their current capability.

You are evaluating how well the teacher feedback achieves a specific criterion of
productive coaching.

Criterion of productive coaching: Is Appropriate Emotional Pitch

Definition: Is the tone appropriate and constructive for the student?

Meets criterion:
The feedback uses neutral, professional language that is supportive even when pointing out areas for improvement, and that addresses the work rather than the student.

Does NOT meet criterion:
Feedback that is harsh, dismissive, or shaming ('this makes no sense'); that evaluates the student rather than the work ('this is a weak response,' 'you clearly didn't read the text'); or that uses praise so inflated or exaggerated that it misrepresents the quality of the work ('this is excellent' on an incomplete response).

Key features to assess independently (each must be judged met=1 or not-met=0 with a
justification grounded in this specific student response and teacher feedback):
  - neutral_professional_language: Whether the language is neutral and professional versus harsh or dismissive
  - targets_work_not_student: Whether the feedback targets the work versus judging the student as a person
  - praise_proportionate_to_work: Whether any praise matches the actual quality of the work versus overstating it

Task scope:
- Review the student response and the teacher feedback.
- Judge whether the teacher feedback meets the criterion described above.
- Assess each key feature above independently.
- Provide an overall answer of 1 if the teacher feedback meets the criterion, and 0
  otherwise.
- Score only what is written, not what you think was meant.
