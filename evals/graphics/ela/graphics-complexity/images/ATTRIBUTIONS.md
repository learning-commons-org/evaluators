# Image attributions

Every image in `images/` is a screenshot taken from a published source and cropped to the
graphic in question. The files are otherwise unmodified: they are the exact bytes the evaluator
reads, so re-encoding or resizing them would change what the model sees.

They are committed here so that `fixtures.json` is reproducible without a network call.
